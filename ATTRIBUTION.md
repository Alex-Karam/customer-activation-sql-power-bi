The DAX files were transcribed from the project workflow, not exported directly from the binary PBIX model. The model diagram documents the verified setup; it is not a Power BI Model view screenshot. The report images are rendered pages of the PDF, not evidence of live interaction by themselves. The validation record distinguishes those outputs from the interactions tested locally.
